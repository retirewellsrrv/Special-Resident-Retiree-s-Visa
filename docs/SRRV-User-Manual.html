1. About This Manual

1.1 Purpose and audience

This manual describes how to use the SRRV Service Platform from the very first click to the final approval of an application. It is written for three groups of readers:

  • Applicants — foreign retirees applying for a Special Resident Retiree’s Visa. Read Part A.
  • Administrators — staff who review consultations, documents, payments and applications. Read Part B.
  • Super Administrators — the owner account that creates and controls admin accounts. Read Part C.

Sections 7 to 10 are reference material for all readers, and are the sections that support staff will use most often once the system is live. Appendix D collects a small number of housekeeping items intended for the technical team rather than for day-to-day users.

1.2 How to read the rules boxes

Throughout the manual, three kinds of boxes appear. They describe conditions that the software enforces — not suggestions. If a condition is not met, the system refuses the action and shows the message quoted in the box.

Rule. A condition the system checks and enforces. Example: a consultation can only be accepted after the consultation fee has been paid.
Note. Helpful behaviour worth knowing — such as the application form saving your progress if the browser tab is closed.
Caution. An action that cannot easily be undone, or a state that locks a record so it can no longer be edited.

2. System Overview

2.1 What the platform does

The SRRV Service Platform is a web portal that carries a retiree’s visa application from first enquiry to approval. It replaces e-mail-and-spreadsheet handling with a single record per applicant: one consultation, one application, a set of uploaded documents, and a payment history — all visible to both the applicant and the staff who process it.

The public part of the site (Services, Packages, FAQs, About, Contact) is open to anyone. Everything else sits behind a login and is restricted by role.

2.2 The three portals and who may enter them

A user’s role decides which portal they land in after logging in, and the system actively blocks entry to the other portals. Attempting to open a portal you do not belong to silently redirects you back to your own dashboard.

RoleLanding page after loginWhat the role can do
Applicant
Created by self-registration		 /applicant/dashboard Request a consultation, pay fees, submit and edit their own SRRV application, upload and re-upload documents, view receipts and notifications, edit their profile.
Admin
Created by a Super Admin		 /admin/dashboard Review all consultations, applications, documents and payments; change statuses; maintain client profiles and service packages.
Super Admin
Provisioned in the database		 /super-admin/dashboard Everything an Admin can do, plus creating, disabling and deleting admin accounts, and viewing the full client register.
Rule — access control. Visiting any page under /applicant, /admin or /super-admin without being logged in redirects you to the login page, remembering where you were headed so you are returned there after signing in.
Rule — disabled admins. An admin whose account has been de-activated by the Super Admin cannot log in. The attempt is refused and the session is signed out immediately.

3. Before You Begin

3.1 Requirements

ItemRequirement
Web browserAny current desktop or mobile browser (Chrome, Edge, Safari, Firefox). The portal is responsive and usable on a phone, though the application form is more comfortable on a laptop.
E-mail addressMandatory and must be reachable — the account cannot be used until the confirmation link is clicked. One account per e-mail address.

3.2 Fees charged by the platform

Two fees are collected inside the portal. They are separate transactions with separate receipts, and each is tied to a different stage of the journey.

FeeAmountWhen it is chargedWhat it unlocks
Consultation fee₱50.00Immediately after the consultation request form is submitted.Allows staff to process and accept the consultation request.
Application fee₱300.00Immediately after the SRRV application form is submitted.Is a pre-condition for the application to be approved.
Note. These two amounts are the platform’s own service charges. Government and third-party costs — medical examination, police clearance, Bureau of Immigration clearance, the PRA visa deposit and so on — are paid outside the portal, and proof of those payments is uploaded as part of the document checklist.
Caution. The portal permits only one active payment per applicant per fee type. If a checkout is abandoned and later retried, the earlier unpaid invoice is cancelled automatically so the same fee can never be charged twice.

Part A — The Applicant’s Journey

Sections A1 to A13 follow one applicant from an empty browser window to an approved application. Follow them in order; the platform will not let you skip ahead.

A1. Creating an account (Register)

  1. Open the site and choose Register (direct address: /register).
  2. Complete every field of the registration form. The rules the system applies are listed in the table below.
  3. Choose a password of at least eight characters and repeat it in Confirm password.
  4. Submit the form. You are taken to a confirmation-pending page and an e-mail is sent to the address you entered.
FieldRequiredRule enforced
First nameYesAt least two characters; must not contain digits. Capitalisation is normalised automatically.
SurnameYesAt least two characters; must not contain digits.
SuffixNoFree text (Jr., III, and similar). Appended to the display name after a comma.
E-mailYesMust be a valid address and must not already belong to a confirmed account.
SexNoMale or female.
BirthdayYesUsed to calculate your age automatically; you never type an age.
NationalityYesStored with normalised capitalisation.
Phone numberYesMust not be blank.
PasswordYesMinimum of eight characters.
Confirm passwordYesMust match the password exactly, otherwise “Passwords do not match”.
Rule — duplicate e-mail addresses. If the address already belongs to a confirmed account, registration is refused with “An account with this email already exists.” If it belongs to an account that was never confirmed, the system quietly re-sends the confirmation link instead of refusing, and takes you to the confirmation page.

A2. Confirming your e-mail address

  1. Open the e-mail from the platform and click the confirmation link. Check the spam folder if it has not arrived within a few minutes.
  2. The link returns you to the site, verifies the account and signs you in.
  3. If the link has expired or was lost, return to the confirmation page and request that it be re-sent.
Rule. Logging in before confirming does not fail with an error — the system re-sends the confirmation e-mail and returns you to the confirmation-pending page. The account stays unusable until the link is clicked.

A3. Logging in

  1. Go to /login and enter your e-mail address and password.
  2. Submit. The system identifies your role and sends you to the matching dashboard — applicants to /applicant/dashboard.
Note. If you tried to open a protected page before logging in, you are returned to that page automatically after a successful sign-in.

A4. Forgotten password

  1. On the login page choose Forgot password and enter your registered e-mail address.
  2. If the address is not on file the system replies “No account found with this email.” — no e-mail is sent.
  3. If it is on file, a reset link is e-mailed to you. Click it; you are taken to the change-password page with an active session.
  4. Enter a new password of at least eight characters and save. You are returned to the login page to sign in with the new password.
Caution. The reset link creates a temporary session. If you leave the page without setting a new password, request a fresh link — the message “No active session. Please request a new password reset link.” means the link has already lapsed.

A5. The applicant dashboard

The dashboard at /applicant/dashboard is the home screen for everything that follows. It shows, in one view:

  • Application status and code — the reference beginning SRRV- that identifies your file.
  • Progress of the review — where the application currently sits.
  • Your documents — each with its own review status, and a re-upload control on any document that needs attention.
  • Payments — the consultation payment and the application payment, each with amount, method, transaction code and status; a Retry payment button appears when a fee is still unpaid.
  • Your concierge contacts — the active staff members assigned to help you.

The left-hand menu gives access to Dashboard, Consultation, Application, Receipts and Profile, with a bell icon for notifications.

A6. Requesting a consultation

The consultation is the compulsory first step: no application may be started until a consultation has been requested, paid for and accepted by staff.

  1. Open Consultation in the menu (/applicant/consultation).
  2. Select a mode of communication: Zoom, Google Meet, WhatsApp call, phone call, or a face-to-face meeting.
  3. Choose a preferred date. Earlier dates cannot be selected — the date must be today or later.
  4. Describe the purpose of the consultation in the free-text box. It must not be empty.
  5. Press Submit Request. The request is saved, staff are notified by e-mail and in the portal, and you are sent straight to the secure checkout page to pay the ₱50 consultation fee.
Rule — one consultation per applicant. Each account holds a single consultation record. Submitting the form again updates that record rather than creating a second one; the button then reads Update Consultation.
Caution — the record locks. Once staff move your consultation to processing or accepted, the form is replaced by a read-only notice and no further edits are possible. Editing is only available while the consultation is pending or rejected.

A7. Paying the consultation fee

  1. After submitting the consultation you are redirected automatically to the hosted checkout page of the payment provider.
  2. Choose a payment method and complete the payment there. The portal never sees or stores your card or wallet details.
  3. On success you are returned to a receipt page showing the transaction code, date, description, amount and a Paid mark.
  4. Choose Return to Dashboard to continue.
Note. When you merely edit an already-paid consultation, no new invoice is raised — you are returned to the dashboard with a success message. A fresh invoice is created only when the previous payment did not succeed.

A8. Payment outcomes and retrying

OutcomeWhat you seeWhat to do
successReceipt page, and the payment appears as paid on the dashboard.Nothing. Wait for staff to act on your consultation.
pendingCheckout was opened but never completed.Use Retry payment on the dashboard. The unpaid invoice is cancelled and a new one is issued.
failedFailure page; the invoice expired or was declined.Use Retry payment.

Rule — no double charging. Retrying is refused with “Payment has already been completed” if a successful payment for that fee already exists. Whenever a retry is allowed, any older unpaid invoice is expired first, so a stale checkout page left open in another tab can no longer be paid.

A9. Waiting for the consultation decision

Staff review the request and set one of three outcomes. You are informed both by e-mail and by an in-portal notification, and the consultation page reflects the change.

DecisionMeaning for you
processingStaff have taken up your request and are arranging the meeting. The form is now locked.
acceptedYour consultation is confirmed. This is the moment the SRRV application form unlocks.
rejectedThe request was declined. You may edit the consultation and submit it again.
Rule. Attempting to open the application form before your consultation is accepted shows “Consultation Required” (if you have never requested one) or “Consultation Submitted” (if one is awaiting a decision), with a button leading back to the consultation page.

A10. Filling in the SRRV application

The application at /applicant/application is a five-step form. Steps 1 to 3 collect information, step 4 is a review of everything you entered, and step 5 is the read-only view of a submitted application.

  3. At step 4, read the summary carefully and use Back to correct anything that is wrong.
  4. Press Submit Application. A confirmation dialogue appears — confirm to proceed, or choose Review Again.
  5. The application is saved with a reference code of the form SRRV-…, staff are notified, and you are taken to the checkout page for the ₱300 application fee.
Note — your progress is saved. Entries and uploaded files are held in the browser as you type, so closing the tab by accident does not lose your work. Reopening the form restores the step you were on.
Rule — missing documents send you back. If a required document is missing when you try to submit from step 4, the form jumps back to step 3 and marks what is missing.

A11. Paying the application fee

  1. You are redirected to the hosted checkout page immediately after submission.
  2. Pay ₱300 by any offered method. A receipt page confirms the transaction on success.
  3. If you abandon or fail the payment, open the dashboard and use Retry payment in the payment card. The rules in A8 apply identically.
Caution. An application is never approved while its fee is unpaid. Staff are blocked from approving it, so an unpaid fee stalls the entire file.

A12. Following the review and re-uploading documents

Staff examine each document individually and may attach a note explaining a decision. Your dashboard shows the status of every document.

  1. On the dashboard, find the document marked rejected or action need and choose the re-upload control.
  2. Select the corrected file. The old file is removed and replaced.
  3. The document returns to pending and re-enters the review queue.

The application itself moves through its own statuses, and each change reaches you as a notification and an e-mail:

Application statusMeaning

Rule — when editing is possible. An application can only be edited while it is pending or rejected. At any other status the attempt is refused with “Your application cannot be modified at this stage.” Re-submitting an edited application returns it to pending.

A13. Notifications, receipts and profile

  • Notifications (bell icon, or /applicant/notifications) — every consultation and application status change is recorded here and links to the page it concerns. Items can be marked as read, individually or all at once, and cleared.
  • Receipts (/applicant/receipts) — the full payment history: transaction code, amount, method, type of fee and status, newest first. Individual receipts can be reopened.
  • Profile (/applicant/profile) — name, sex, birthday, nationality and civil status. Your age is recalculated from the birthday whenever you save. Names and nationality must not contain digits.

Part B — The Administrator’s Journey

Part B describes the staff side of the same journey: the queues, the decisions available at each stage, and the conditions the system enforces before it will let a decision be recorded.

B1. Signing in and the admin dashboard

Admins sign in at the same /login page as applicants and are routed to /admin/dashboard. The sidebar offers Dashboard, Client Profiles, Consultations, Applications, Payments, Documents and Services.

The dashboard summarises the whole operation: applications by status, revenue collected and revenue by month, payments succeeded, pending and refunded, documents awaiting review, consultations by status, total registered users, and lists of the most recent applications, consultations and pending documents.

B2. Consultations queue — accepting or rejecting

  1. Open Consultations. The header shows counts of total, pending, processing, accepted and rejected requests.
  2. Filter by status or search by the applicant’s name; results are paginated ten to a page, newest first. A badge marks consultations that already have an application attached.
  3. Open a request to see the applicant’s name and e-mail, preferred date, mode of communication, purpose, the consultation payment, and the linked application if one exists.
  4. Set the status to Processing, Accepted or Rejected.
Rule — payment first. A consultation cannot be set to processing or accepted until its ₱50 fee has been paid. The attempt is refused with “Cannot accept: the consultation fee has not been paid yet.” Rejecting is always available.
Note. Accepting stamps the record with your identity and the time of acceptance, and the detail page then shows “Accepted by …”. If the consultation is later moved out of accepted, that stamp is cleared.
Caution. Accepting a consultation is what unlocks the SRRV application form for that applicant. Do not accept until the consultation has genuinely taken place or been agreed.

B3. Applications queue — reviewing and approving

  1. Open Applications. Counts of total, paused, pending, approved and rejected are shown at the top.
  2. Filter by status, or search by applicant name or application code.
  3. Open an application to see the complete file: personal details, passport, entry visa, education, employment, dependants, family background, contact and emergency contact, the linked consultation, the application payment, and every uploaded document.
  4. Review each document (section B4) before deciding on the application as a whole.
  5. Set the application status. Every change notifies the applicant in the portal and by e-mail.
Rule — five conditions must hold before an application can be approved. The system checks each in turn and names the first one that fails:
  1. Contact details are complete — mobile number and home-country address are both present.
  2. A successful application payment exists (the consultation fee does not count).
  3. Every uploaded document carries the status accepted; otherwise it reports how many are outstanding.
  4. The applicant profile section of the application is present.
  5. Passport information is present.
Rule — setting an application to processing requires the mobile number and home-country address to be present.
Note. Approving stamps the file with the approver and the timestamp; both are cleared if the application later leaves the approved state. Setting a status to the value it already holds is ignored, so the applicant is never sent a duplicate notification.

B4. Document review

Documents can be reviewed either inside an application, or in bulk from the Documents queue, which lists every uploaded file across all applications with counts by status.

  1. Open the document to view it. Viewing generates a temporary secure link that expires after one hour.
  2. Set its status: pending, processing, accepted, rejected or action need.
  3. When rejecting or asking for action, write a review note. The applicant reads this note and it is the only guidance they receive.
  4. Where several documents share the same outcome, select them and apply the status in one operation.
Note. When an applicant re-uploads, the replacement file overwrites the old one and the status returns to pending, so it reappears in your queue.

B5. Payments monitoring

The Payments page lists every transaction with the client’s name, amount, status, method, transaction code, fee type and date, newest first. Filters cover status, method and fee type, and you can search by transaction code, client name or method. Summary figures show total revenue, revenue split between application and consultation fees, counts of successful and pending payments, and the number and value of refunded (cancelled) payments.

Note. Payment records are written by the payment provider’s callback, not by hand. Statuses on this page are a reflection of the gateway, which is why there is no control to edit them.

B6. Client profiles

Client Profiles is the client register: name, sex, birthday, nationality, age, civil status, and the state of that client’s application. Opening a client shows their full record including the approved-application detail where it exists. Admins may correct profile details (the no-digits rules apply here too), return an application to pending so it can be reviewed again, and set the application status directly from the client’s record.

B7. Services and packages

The Services page maintains the service plans shown to the public on the Services and Packages pages. Admins can create a plan, edit it, delete it, mark one plan as featured, and switch a plan’s availability on or off. Changes appear on the public site.

B8. Admin notifications

The bell icon collects staff notifications. Two events raise them, and they are sent to every active admin:

  • A new or updated consultation request — linking to the consultations queue.
  • A new or re-submitted application — linking to that applicant’s application.

Notifications can be marked read individually or all at once, and cleared. The same two events also generate e-mails to the administrative mailbox, as does a paid consultation fee.

Part C — The Super Administrator

The Super Admin account owns the system. It can do everything an admin can do, and is the only role that can create or remove admin accounts.

C1. Dashboard

Signing in as Super Admin lands on /super-admin/dashboard, with menu entries for Dashboard, Admins and Clients.

C2. Managing admin accounts

On Admins (/super-admin/manage-admins) every admin is listed with name, e-mail address, whether the account is active, whether the e-mail has been confirmed, and the date it was created.

Creating an admin

  1. Choose New Admin and enter the name, e-mail address and an initial password.
  2. Submit. All three fields are required and the password must be at least eight characters.
  3. The account is created as active, and a confirmation e-mail is sent. The message “Admin created. They need to check their email to confirm their account.” confirms this.
  4. The new admin clicks the link in that e-mail, then signs in at /login.
Rule. If the e-mail address already belongs to a confirmed user, creation is refused with “A user with this email already exists.” If it belongs to an unconfirmed user, that account is updated with the new password and name and the confirmation e-mail is sent again.

Disabling and deleting admins

  • Disable — switch the account to inactive. The person can no longer log in, stops receiving staff notifications, and no longer appears to applicants as a concierge contact. The account and its history are retained, and it can be switched back on at any time.
  • Delete — removes the admin profile and the underlying login permanently.
Caution. Deletion cannot be undone. Where a member of staff has simply left or is temporarily away, disable the account rather than deleting it.

C3. Viewing client accounts

Clients (/super-admin/manage-clients) is a read-only register of every applicant: name, e-mail, nationality, sex, age, civil status, the status and code of their application, and the date they registered.

8. How Payments Work Behind the Scenes

This section is for the staff who will answer payment questions. It explains why the portal behaves as it does when a payment goes wrong.

8.1 The path of a payment

  1. The applicant submits a consultation or an application.
  2. Before anything is charged, the portal checks that its own payment callback and its success and failure pages are reachable. If they are not, no invoice is created and the applicant is told “Payment processing is unavailable… Please contact support.” This prevents a payment being taken that the portal could not then record.
  3. Any earlier unpaid invoice for the same fee is expired and marked failed.
  4. A payment record is written with a unique transaction code beginning srrv- and the status pending.
  5. An invoice is raised with the payment provider and the applicant is redirected to the hosted checkout page.
  6. When the payment concludes, the provider calls back to the portal, and that callback — not the applicant’s browser — is what sets the final status. A payment marked paid becomes success; expired or failed invoices become failed.

8.2 The safeguards

SafeguardWhat it prevents
One active payment per applicant per feeTwo open or two successful invoices for the same fee cannot exist at the same time.
Old invoices expired before a retryA checkout page left open in another tab cannot be paid after a new invoice is issued.
Only pending payments are updated by the callbackA late “expired” message cannot downgrade a payment that already succeeded.
Duplicate success detectionIf a second invoice is somehow paid, it is recorded as failed rather than counted twice in revenue.
Signed callbackCallbacks that do not carry the agreed secret token are rejected outright.
Note for support staff. Because the final status comes from the provider’s callback, a payment can briefly appear as pending on the dashboard even though the applicant has just paid. Refreshing after a few moments is the correct advice; a payment that is still pending after several minutes should be checked against the provider’s own dashboard using the transaction code.

10. Troubleshooting — Messages and What They Mean

10.1 Messages an applicant may see

MessageCause and remedy
“An account with this email already exists.”The address belongs to a confirmed account. Log in instead, or use the forgotten-password flow.
“Passwords do not match”The two password boxes differ. Re-type both.
“Password must be at least 8 characters.”Choose a longer password.
“No account found with this email.”The reset was requested for an address that is not registered. Check the spelling.
“No active session. Please request a new password reset link.”The reset link has lapsed. Request a new one.
“Preferred date must be today or in the future”A past date was chosen for the consultation.
“Your consultation is currently being processed and can no longer be modified.”Staff have moved the consultation to processing or accepted. Contact your concierge for changes.
“Your consultation request must be accepted before you can submit an application.”Wait for the consultation decision. If it has not been paid, pay the fee first.
“Your application cannot be modified at this stage.”The application is no longer pending or rejected. Contact your concierge.
“Payment has already been completed”The fee is already paid; no second invoice will be issued.
“Payment processing is unavailable… Please contact support.”The portal could not verify its payment callbacks. This is a configuration or connectivity problem — report it to the technical team.
“Passport is expired or expires today”The passport validity date must be later than today.
“Invalid passport number format”Use 5 to 20 characters, capital letters and digits only.
“All fields in this row must be filled”An education, employment or dependant row is partly filled. Complete it or clear it entirely.
“Receipt not found.”The reference in the address does not match a payment. Check the dashboard for the current status.
“Invalid file format. Accepted: PDF, DOC, DOCX, JPG, PNG, GIF, BMP, WEBP, TIFF.”Convert the file and upload again.

10.2 Messages an administrator may see

MessageCause and remedy
“Cannot accept: the consultation fee has not been paid yet.”Ask the applicant to pay, or to retry the payment from their dashboard.
“Cannot approve: missing required contact fields (Phone Number, Address)”The application lacks a mobile number or home-country address. Ask the applicant to edit and re-submit.
“Cannot approve: no successful payment found”The ₱300 application fee is unpaid.
“Cannot approve: N document(s) not yet accepted”Finish reviewing every document first.
“Cannot approve: applicant profile is missing” / “passport information is missing”The submission is incomplete; have the applicant edit and re-submit.
“A user with this email already exists.”Creating an admin on an address that already belongs to a confirmed user.
Login refused for an adminThe account has been de-activated by the Super Admin. Re-activate it on the Admins page.

10.3 First checks when something looks wrong

  1. An applicant cannot start their application. Check the consultation: does it exist, is the fee paid, and is it accepted?
  2. An application cannot be approved. Work down the five conditions in section 7.6 in order.
  3. A payment says pending but the client says they paid. Wait a moment and refresh; then match the transaction code against the payment provider’s dashboard.
  4. A client is not receiving e-mail. Check the spam folder, confirm the address on the profile, and check the e-mail audit log.
  5. An admin sees nothing in their notification bell. Only active admins receive staff notifications.
